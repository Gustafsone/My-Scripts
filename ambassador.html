Bookshelf Access Instructions for Safari Users

Follow the steps below to access your course materials through Bookshelf using Safari.

  1. Go to https://mobapbookstore.com
  2. Login to your account
  3. Select Access EA Program
  4. Select the eBooks tab
  5. Select Access eBooks
  6. A new tab will open up. Enter in your Student Email to create an account.
    • Enter a password. Be sure to memorize it.
    • Verify your account
  7. After verifying your account, select My Library, and you should see your materials.
  8. You can access your materials through Canvas now using the Bookshelf link and logging in with the account you just created.

Congratulations! You have set up access to your Bookshelf materials!

Important Note: For optimal compatibility with Canvas and all integrated course materials, we recommend using Chrome browser instead of Safari whenever possible.

Bookshelf Access Instructions for Canvas

Use this method unless the student is using Safari on a MacBook or reports a Canvas Bookshelf launch problem.

  1. Open any Canvas course at https://mobap.instructure.com
  2. Select the Bookshelf link in the left-hand course navigation.
  3. If prompted, create a Bookshelf account using an email address and validate that email.
  4. On the Bookshelf home screen, select Home, then My Library.
    • A new tab opens with all available materials.

Congratulations! You have set up access to your Bookshelf materials!

Follow the steps below to access your access codes in the bookstore.

Instructions for Ordering your Lab Kit via the Bookstore

Follow the steps below to order a lab kit voucher code from the bookstore.

  1. Go to https://mobapbookstore.com
  2. Login to your account
  3. Select Order Lab Kit
  4. Select the New Order button from the top of the page
    • Your Lab courses will be preselected for you.
    • Confirm the course(s) are correct and select Continue
  5. Choose the lab kit to add to it your cart and select Continue
  6. Review your cart and shipping address and finalize the order
  7. Note: you can't change your shipping address. Nothing physical will be shipped to you from the bookstore.
  8. After your order is complete, you will receive order confirmation, and the voucher code will be emailed to you.
  9. After you receive the voucher code, you will access the publisher website to provide your code and shipping address

You can also purchase a lab kit directly from the publisher's website. The tradeoff is you have to provide payment information directly. Ordering from the bookstore passes the charge to your student account that you can use Financial Aid for.

Access for Physical Materials

For rental textbook, lab manuals, or other physical materials, follow the steps below:

  1. Go to https://mobapbookstore.com
  2. Login to your account
  3. Select Access EA Program
  4. On the right-hand side, select the Verify Address link under the Action Required section.
  5. Choose the address you want to ship the materials to. After you validate your address, an order will be created, and you will see shipping information shortly after.

Congratulations! You have set validated your address for physical materials.

Returning Rental Textbooks

At the end of the semester, you will receive an email reminding you to return your textbook rentals to the bookstore. If you kept the box they were shipped in you may use that box. The email will contain a return address label. Print off the label, pack the books in the box, affix the label, and drop it off at a UPS location.

The return shipment must be marked as received at the UPS store by the due date, otherwise, you will be charged the full price of the textbook.

If you have issues with returning materials, please log into your bookstore account and locate the Help Center.

You may return a rental textbook at any time by logging in to your bookstore account, finding the order for the textbook, and initiating a return. You will be given a return label via email that you need to print off and use to make sure the return gets credited to your account.

Publisher Access Code Redemption Instructions

Almost all of MBU's Canvas courses integrate publisher material through Canvas, which requires students to redeem their access codes through Canvas rather than the publisher website. Bookshelf will tell students to go to the publisher website to redeem the access code. The publisher website often asks students for a course key or class code. These do not exist since the course is integrated through Canvas. Direct all students to go back into Canvas to redeem their access codes.

ALEKS

Follow the steps below to access and register in ALEKS.

  1. Copy the code from Bookshelf
  2. Select ALEKS in the course navigation
  3. You will be asked if you have used ALEKS before.
    • If you have, select Yes and login with your username and password
    • If you have not, select No and create a username and password
  4. Select continue
  5. You will be given option for access. Enter in the access code from the Bookstore or purchase a code.
  6. You will then be taken to your My Classes page.

The first thing you will do when entering your course is to complete the Initial Knowledge Check.

Congratulations! You have registered in ALEKS!
