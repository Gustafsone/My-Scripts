Our mission comes first.

As a Christ-centered institution, MBU offers this resource only insofar as it serves our mission. AI is a tool that can support learning, but it cannot replace the intellectual, ethical and spiritual formation at the heart of a Christian education. We encourage every student, faculty and staff member to use these tools with wisdom, discernment, integrity and stewardship, in ways that honor God and serve others. Where the use of AI tools conflicts with our Christian commitments or University policies, those commitments take priority.

Missouri Baptist University does not endorse Google or its viewpoints. Any perspective encountered in these courses that conflicts with the University's beliefs does not represent an official position of the University.

Using AI responsibly

  • Do not enter personal, confidential or proprietary information into Gemini or other generative AI tools.
  • Do not include FERPA-protected student data or grades in prompts.
  • Treat AI output as a starting point, not a final answer — verify what matters.
  • Follow MBU's academic integrity policy in all coursework, whether or not AI is involved.

FAQ

Common questions

Is there really no cost?

Correct — access to Google Career Certificates, AI Essentials, the AI Professional Certificate, Gemini and Gemini Notebook is free to current MBU students, faculty and staff through this partnership. There is no cost to you or to the University.

Who is eligible to enroll?

Current MBU students, faculty and staff. Access is limited to a shared pool of licenses, so enrollment may be managed as demand grows.

Is my data safe when I use Gemini?

Treat Gemini like any other generative AI tool: avoid entering personal, confidential or FERPA-protected information into your prompts. See "Using AI Responsibly" above for full guidance.

How long does a certificate take to complete?

Certificates are self-paced and flexible. Most learners complete a certificate in a few months studying a few hours a week, though pace is entirely up to you.
